Save Lumina to your Home Screen

Lumina runs in the browser, so there is nothing in the App Store. Saving it to the home screen gives it an icon and opens it full-screen, without the address bar — worth doing on the phone that will be the booth.

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. 1

    Open the app in Safari

    Go to luminabooth-app.web.app.

  2. 2

    Tap Share

    The square with an arrow pointing up, in Safari’s toolbar.

  3. 3

    Tap Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the list if you do not see it straight away. Then tap Add.

  4. 4

    Open Lumina from its icon

    The first time, you will be asked to sign in again, even if you were signed in in Safari. After that it remembers you.

On Android

  1. 1

    Open the app in Chrome

    Go to luminabooth-app.web.app.

  2. 2

    Open the menu

    The three dots at the top right.

  3. 3

    Tap Add to Home screen

    On some phones it says Install app instead. Confirm, and the icon appears with your other apps.
