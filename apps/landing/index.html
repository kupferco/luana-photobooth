How it works

  1. 1

    Stand a phone on a tripod

    Open Lumina on it and tap Use this phone as the booth. Plug the printer in beside it. That is the whole rig.

  2. 2

    Guests scan the code

    A QR on the table opens on their own phone. They join the queue there, so nobody stands around at the booth waiting for a turn.

  3. 3

    Three shots, one print

    The booth counts them in and takes three. Moments later it is a 6×4 print in their hand and a link on their phone.

What you get

Prints on the spot

Six by four inches, on a dye-sublimation printer, dry to the touch as it comes out. The thing people actually take home.

No queue at the booth

Guests trigger a session from their own phone and the booth calls them up. One booth keeps a long line moving.

A link that still works

Every photo gets its own page. No app, no account, nothing to sign in to — it opens weeks later on anyone’s phone.

Gone in ninety days

Photos are deleted automatically after three months. Guests can also delete their own, at the booth or from their phone.

Your artwork on it

Drop in a background for the party. The layout leaves a corner free for it, so the print looks like yours and not like a template.

A second pair of hands

Invite someone to help run one party. They see that party and nothing else on your account.

What you need

Lumina is the software. You bring a phone; the printing kit is rented from us, in London.

  • A phone on a tripod, to be the booth. Yours.
  • A Canon SELPHY photo printer. Ours, rented.
  • A Raspberry Pi — a small box beside the printer that drives it. Ours, rented with the printer.

The Pi joins your wifi from a card taped to the box — no keyboard, no screen, no typing an IP address.

Questions

Do I have to buy a printer?

No. We rent out the kit: a Canon SELPHY printer and the Raspberry Pi that drives it, already set up to work together. You supply the phone and the tripod.

Where can I rent the kit?

In London, for now. The app itself works anywhere, but without the kit there are no prints — guests still get their photos as a link.

Do guests need to install anything?

No. They scan the QR code and it opens in their phone’s browser. No app, no account.

Do I need to install an app?

No, Lumina runs in the browser. It is nicer full-screen, though, especially on the phone that is the booth — save it to your home screen.

Does the venue need wifi?

Yes. The printer box needs a wifi network with a password you know, and the booth phone needs to be online. A phone hotspot does the job where the venue has none. Wifi that makes you sign in on a web page first, as some hotels do, will not work for the printer.

How long does setting up take?

A few minutes once you have the wifi password: plug in, join the printer’s setup network, type in a code. The setup guide has every step.

How big are the prints, and how fast?

Six by four inches, with three photos on each. A print takes about a minute, and they come out one at a time in the order people posed.

What if the printer runs out of paper?

Nothing is lost. Every photo is saved as it is taken and the guest gets their link either way. Your party screen shows the printer’s state, and you can print any photo again from there once it is refilled.

What happens to the photos afterwards?

They are kept for 90 days after the party and then deleted. You can download the lot before then, and any guest can delete their own sooner.

Can the prints have our own design?

Yes. Upload a background for the party — landscape, 3:2, ideally 1800×1200 — and it sits behind the photos on every print.
