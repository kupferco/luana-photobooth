How it works

  1. 1

    Stand a phone on a tripod

    Open Lumina on it and tap Use this phone as the booth. Plug the printer in beside it. That is the whole rig.

  2. 2

    Guests scan the code

    A QR on the table opens on their own phone. They join the queue there, so nobody stands around at the booth waiting for a turn.

  3. 3

    Three shots, one print

    The booth counts them in and takes three. Moments later it is a 6×4 print in their hand and a link on their phone.

What you get

Prints on the spot

Six by four inches, on a dye-sublimation printer, dry to the touch as it comes out. The thing people actually take home.

No queue at the booth

Guests trigger a session from their own phone and the booth calls them up. One booth keeps a long line moving.

A link that still works

Every photo gets its own page. No app, no account, nothing to sign in to — it opens weeks later on anyone’s phone.

Gone in ninety days

Photos are deleted automatically after three months. Guests can also delete their own, at the booth or from their phone.

Your artwork on it

Drop in a background for the party. The layout leaves a corner free for it, so the print looks like yours and not like a template.

A second pair of hands

Invite someone to help run one party. They see that party and nothing else on your account.

What you need

Lumina is the software. The kit is ordinary and mostly reusable:

  • A phone on a tripod, to be the booth.
  • A Canon SELPHY photo printer, and paper.
  • A Raspberry Pi beside the printer, to drive it.

The Pi joins your wifi from a card taped to the box — no keyboard, no screen, no typing an IP address.

Run one this weekend

Sign in with your email. There is no password to make up and nothing to install.
