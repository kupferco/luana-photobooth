Setting up the booth

From the kit in your hands to the first print. Allow a quarter of an hour, and do it at the venue, on the wifi you will use on the day.

What is in the kit

The kit is rented from Lumina, in London. It holds three things:

  • A Canon SELPHY — the photo printer. It makes the 6×4 prints.
  • A Raspberry Pi — the small box wired to the printer. It fetches each photo and hands it to the SELPHY. It has no screen and no buttons you need.
  • A tripod — to hold the booth phone sideways.

On the box is a card with a QR code and a three-word name, something like popcorn-sherbet-dance. You will need both.

What you bring

  • A phone to be the booth, and its charger.
  • The wifi name and password for the venue.
  • Your own phone, to do the setting up from.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Create the party in the app

    On your own phone, open the app, sign in with your email and tap New event. Give it a name and a date.

  2. 2

    Plug in the kit

    Plug in the Pi and the SELPHY, check the cable between them is in, and turn the printer on. Make sure it has paper and ink loaded. Give the Pi a minute or two to start.

  3. 3

    Get a pairing code

    In your event, under Booth and printer, tap Connect a printer, then Set up a new printer. The app shows a pairing code. It lasts 15 minutes and works once; tap it to copy.

  4. 4

    Join the printer’s own network

    In your phone’s wifi settings, join the network with the three-word name from the card. It has no password. This is a temporary network the Pi makes just for setting up.

  5. 5

    Scan the card

    Scan the QR code on the card, or type lumina.local into your browser. Choose the venue’s wifi, enter its password, and paste the pairing code.

  6. 6

    Watch the setup network disappear

    The Pi joins the venue’s wifi and its own network vanishes. That is what success looks like. Your phone goes back to its usual wifi, and the printer shows as connected in your event.

  7. 7

    Stand the booth phone on the tripod

    Sideways — the booth takes landscape photos. Plug it in to charge. Open the app on it, sign in, open the event and tap Use this phone as the booth. Leave that screen open. Saving the app to the home screen first makes it full-screen.

  8. 8

    Start the event and take a test photo

    Tap Start the event, then tap the booth screen and pose. Three shots, and about a minute later a print. If it comes out, you are done.

  9. 9

    Put the QR code on the table

    Print the Guest QR code from your event and stand it where people will see it. Guests scan it to join the queue from their own phone; it is on the booth screen too.

If something is not working

I cannot see the three-word network

Give the Pi a couple of minutes after plugging it in, then look again. If it still is not there, unplug the Pi, plug it back in and wait.

lumina.local will not open

Your phone has probably hopped back to another network. Check in wifi settings that it is still on the three-word one, then try again.

It says the code has expired

Codes last 15 minutes and work once. Go back to the app, make a new one, and enter that.

The setup network came back

The Pi could not join the wifi, nearly always because of a mistyped password. Join the three-word network again and repeat from step 3 with a new code.

The photo was taken but nothing printed

Look at the printer in your event: it shows what the printer itself is reporting. Usually it is paper, ink, or the printer being switched off. Fix that, then print the photo again from the event.

None of that helped

Message us on WhatsApp and say what you are seeing.
